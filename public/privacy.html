Maya privacy

Maya is a voice and chat companion for older people, made by the Maya team. This is a prototype, and this page says plainly what that means for your data.

What Maya collects

  • What a person types to Maya, and the voice notes and photos they send.
  • The profile name and the gender chosen when the family sets someone up.
  • Their WhatsApp phone number, only if the family links it, and only after the person has agreed.
  • The family dashboard settings: mode, reminders, and the person's time zone (not their location).
  • Short notes Maya keeps for each person so she can remember them, such as names, preferences and plans. Each person has their own, kept apart from everyone else's.
  • Alerts to the family record the topic only (for example "a possible scam" or "a fall"). The dashboard never shows the words, audio or photos.

What Maya does not collect

  • Location.
  • Messages that are not to Maya, or anything from other apps.
  • Phone contacts.
  • Live video.
  • Fall detection or other sensor data. Maya only knows about a fall if the person tells her.

How it is used

  • To talk with the person, and to remember them, per profile.
  • To check a message or photo for signs of a scam, and to tell the person.
  • To tell the family the topic of an alert, when it is needed or the person agrees.
  • Maya never sells data.
  • Maya never gives anyone the exact words, audio, photos or phone numbers, other than the services that make her work, listed next. The family only ever gets the alert topic.

Services that make Maya work. To understand and answer, the words, voice notes and photos go to Meta's AI services. Maya's spoken reply text goes to ElevenLabs to make her voice. When something needs checking, such as a news claim or a company, a short search text may go to a search service, and public company websites may be read. These services only see what is needed for that one step. Their own policies apply to what they receive.

Keeping and deleting

  • When a profile is deleted, everything about that person is deleted with it: their notes, dashboard data, saved voice notes and photos, and the WhatsApp link.
  • The family can also ask for deletion by email: sattiaatish94@gmail.com.
  • Maya does not keep call transcripts. Spoken calls are turned into short notes, and the words are thrown away.
  • A prototype limit, said honestly: Maya currently runs on a free hosting plan whose storage is wiped whenever the service is redeployed or restarted. All profiles and notes can disappear without warning. Do not rely on Maya to keep anything.

Meta and WhatsApp

  • Messages on WhatsApp travel through Meta's WhatsApp Business API. Meta's own policies apply to how WhatsApp delivers them.
  • Maya only answers messages on WhatsApp from a number the family linked and the person agreed to. Replying STOP turns it off.
  • Maya can answer a call from a linked number on WhatsApp, and that call audio goes to the same services as in-app calls.
