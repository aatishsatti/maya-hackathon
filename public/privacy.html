Maya privacy

Maya is a voice and chat companion for older people, made by the Maya team. This is a prototype, and this page says plainly what that means for your data.

What Maya collects

  • What a person types to Maya, what they say on a call (kept as chat lines, not as audio), and the voice notes and photos they send.
  • The profile name and the gender chosen when the family sets someone up.
  • Their WhatsApp phone number, only if the family links it, and only after the person has agreed.
  • The family dashboard settings: mode, reminders, and the person's time zone (not their location).
  • Reminders: what to remind about, when, who set it, and what happened to each one (scheduled, sent, delivered, read, answered or missed).
  • Short notes Maya keeps for each person so she can remember them, such as names, preferences and plans. Each person has their own, kept apart from everyone else's.
  • Alerts to the family record the topic only (for example "a possible scam" or "a fall"). The dashboard never shows the words, audio or photos.
  • If a person says no to Maya telling their family about a possible scam, no message or alert is sent. The topic (never their words or the photo) still appears as a yellow flag on the family dashboard. Saying no stops the notification, not the note on the dashboard.

What Maya does not collect

  • Location.
  • Messages that are not to Maya, or anything from other apps.
  • Phone contacts.
  • Live video.
  • Fall detection or other sensor data. Maya only knows about a fall if the person tells her.

How it is used

  • To talk with the person, and to remember them, per profile.
  • To check a message or photo for signs of a scam, and to tell the person.
  • To tell the family the topic of an alert, when it is needed or the person agrees.
  • To send the reminders the family or the person set, and to show the family honestly whether each one was sent, delivered, read, answered or missed.
  • Maya never sells data.
  • Maya never gives anyone the exact words, audio, photos or phone numbers, other than the services that make her work, listed next. The family only ever gets the alert topic.

Services that make Maya work. To understand and answer, the words, voice notes and photos go to Meta's AI services. Maya's spoken reply text is turned into speech on our server using Meta's open-source MMS text-to-speech model. When a known company is named in a possible scam, its public official website from our allow-list may be read. These services only see what is needed for that one step. Their own policies apply to what they receive.

Keeping and deleting

  • When a profile is deleted, everything about that person is deleted with it: their notes, dashboard data, saved voice notes and photos, and the WhatsApp link.
  • The family can also ask for deletion by email: sattiaatish@gmail.com.
  • Calls are not recorded: the audio of a call is never kept. But what is said on a call is also shown in that person's chat history, so nothing said aloud disappears. The latest 200 lines of chat and calls are kept for each person on our server disk until the profile is deleted, and they are words. After a call, the words are also turned into short notes; the temporary file of words used for that is deleted once the notes are made.
  • Reminders are deleted with the profile. A finished one-time reminder is deleted after 30 days, and a cancelled reminder is deleted at once.
  • Maya's server logs are meant to hold counts and times only, not what anyone said.
  • A prototype limit, said honestly: Maya's data is kept on a persistent disk at our host, but this is still a prototype with no promise of service. Do not rely on Maya for medicine or for anything time-critical.

Meta and WhatsApp

  • Messages on WhatsApp travel through Meta's WhatsApp Business API. Meta's own policies apply to how WhatsApp delivers them.
  • Maya only answers messages on WhatsApp from a number the family linked and the person agreed to. Replying STOP turns it off.
  • Maya can answer a call from a linked number on WhatsApp, and that call audio goes to the same services as in-app calls.

Reminders

  • A reminder is sent on WhatsApp as a text or a voice note in Maya's voice. When WhatsApp's 24-hour window is closed, it goes as an approved template message whose text is the reminder, with a Got it button; if WhatsApp cannot deliver it, it goes to the Maya app instead. The reminder text goes to Meta for WhatsApp delivery, and her voice note is rendered on our server with Meta MMS TTS.
  • The family dashboard shows what happened to each reminder: scheduled, sent, delivered, read, answered or missed. A missed reminder is shown as missed, never hidden.
  • Maya is a companion, not medical care. She gives no doses and no medical advice. A reminder is only a reminder: it can fail or arrive late, so never rely on Maya for medicine or anything time-critical.

Time zone

  • Maya needs to know the time where the person is, so that a reminder "at 8" is at their 8 and nothing is sent at night. She finds it by herself, in this order: the phone or browser tells her its own time zone; failing that, a one-time lookup of where their internet connection is; failing that, the country code of their linked WhatsApp number (only for countries with a single time zone). Only if none of these works does the family dashboard ask for it.
  • The internet-connection lookup happens once, and only while the time zone is still unknown. Their internet address is sent to a public location service (ipapi.co) for that one lookup and is not kept by Maya: only the resulting time zone name is saved. The service sees the address, as any website you visit does.
  • This lookup can be switched off by whoever runs the server. Then the phone's own time zone, the phone number's country and the dashboard are used instead.
