    WhatsApp rules, in plain words

      A reminder goes on WhatsApp only while they have written to Maya in the last 24 hours. Every reminder shows what really happened to it: sent, delivered, read, answered, or missed. Maya is a companion, not medical care: a reminder can fail or arrive late, so never rely on it for medicine.

      WhatsApp

      Call them with a reminder (test)

      Rings their WhatsApp once. Maya says the reminder in her own words, listens, and ends the call herself. Needs their call permission.

      Check in with them (test)

      Maya says it is a welfare check and asks how they are. As a call she then talks with them (needs their call permission); as a text or a voice note she sends the same short words, only while they have written in the last 24 hours. No reminder is used.
